Meet Him

Privacy Policy · Last updated May 1, 2026

Meet Him is a Christian prayer, meditation, and devotional app. Your privacy matters. This policy explains clearly what we collect, what we don't collect, and how your data is handled.

What We Collect

Nothing that leaves your device. Meet Him stores all personal data — journal entries, prayer notes, session history, and voice preferences — exclusively on your device using local storage. This data is never transmitted to any server, never shared with any third party, and never accessed by us.

Journal & Prayer Entries

Everything you write in the Prayer Journal — your reflections, prayers, gratitude entries, and closing thoughts — is stored only on your device. It is private to you. We have no access to it.

Audio Content

Guided meditations, sleep stories, and devotional audio are fetched from a publicly accessible file server (GitHub) when you play them. No personal information is transmitted as part of this request — it is equivalent to loading a web page.

Bible Content

Bible text is fetched from a third-party Bible API (bolls.life) when you navigate to a chapter. No personal information is transmitted as part of this request.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not track how you use the app. We do not sell data. There are no third-party trackers in this app.

Children's Privacy

Meet Him does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no account creation, no social features, and no data collection of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect that. We will not reduce your privacy protections without clear notice.
